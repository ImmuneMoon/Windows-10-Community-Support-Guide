## 📖 Overview
The **Windows 10 Community Support Guide** is for anyone who wants to stay on Windows 10 with the help of a real community. It points to the repair groups, forums, and volunteers who help people keep old PCs running, and it's built around The Restart Project's End of Windows 10 toolkit.

The guide also explains what you give up with a "lite" build such as Windows X-Lite. Those builds can make Windows smaller, but they aren't community support for security: the newest X-Lite update pack is from October 2025, and key protections are removed or switched off. The safest way to stay on Windows 10 is an official copy with Extended Security Updates, and the guide covers how to set that up and what to plan for afterward.

## 🗂 Contents of the Guide
The main document (`Guide.md`) is divided into these sections:
1. **The Short Answer** - The recommendation, for readers who just want to know what to do.
2. **First, Check Whether Your PC Can Run Windows 11** - What to do if your PC qualifies, and your options if it doesn't.
3. **Where to Find Real Community Support** - Repair groups, forums, what to bring to an event, and Linux as the option with a real community behind it.
4. **Where Windows 10 Stands Right Now** - Support dates, costs, and catches for each option, including Extended Security Updates and 0patch.
5. **Stay on Windows 10 the Safe Way** - Step-by-step setup of official Windows 10, from backup to Extended Security Updates and debloating.
6. **Tool Walkthroughs** - Step-by-step use of restore points, O&O ShutUp10++, 0patch, VirtualBox, and Autoruns.
7. **How Lite Builds Actually Work** - The three types of build, and how much trust each one needs.
8. **What You Give Up With Windows X-Lite** - The missing security fixes, each feature that's removed or switched off, and whether you can get it back.
9. **Other Risks of Lite Builds** - Malware, breakage, ESU eligibility, and activation cracks.
10. **If You Still Choose a Lite Build** - How to test a build and limit the damage if it's compromised.
11. **Things That Don't Make It Safer** - Common reassurances that don't prove much.
12. **Quick Checklist** - A short summary to work through.

## ⚠️ Important Disclaimer
This guide isn't legal or security advice, and it doesn't accuse any project of including malware. The details of Windows X-Lite come from the project's own pages and describe one release at one point in time, so check the current version. Support dates and prices also change. Modified Windows isn't supported by Microsoft, and you're responsible for licensing whatever you install. This guide doesn't cover activation workarounds.
